# SHOP-102 — A duck, but make it a gift.

Add optional gift wrapping and a short personal message at checkout.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Gift wrapping is opt-in, costs **€4**, and appears as its own summary line.
2. Wrap is added after duck discounts; the wrapping fee itself is not discounted.
3. Gift message is plain text, at most 120 characters, and saved with the order.
4. A normal unwrapped order behaves as before.

Friendly copy: “For someone who needs a little duck.” No real checkout or email provider is required.
